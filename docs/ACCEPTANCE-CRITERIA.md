# Acceptance criteria

These are behavioral requirements, not a claim that all scenarios have passed. P0 is required for a candidate pilot; P1 is assessed for quality during evaluation. IDs AC01–AC82 preserve the previous specification's scenario identity; AC83–AC106 extend it. The English wording consolidates the contract; archived originals retain its history.

## Targeted audit and executable delivery checks

| ID | Priority | Scenario and observable acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| AC93 | P0 | Revised briefing: ask only consequential missing questions (normally up to 3–5), reuse answers/skips, and show the actual updated strategy in conversation before collection. |
| AC94 | P0 | Decisive claim: reopen original evidence or its original captured bytes, identify exact version/locator and disposition; an agent extraction does not verify itself. |
| AC95 | P0 | Invalid premise: correct or withdraw all dependent conclusions/tables/report variants before assigning confidence; a disclaimer or widened range cannot repair the premise. |
| AC96 | P0 | Quantitative comparison: align denominators/populations/dates, deduplicate or expose sensitivity, separate exploratory samples and verify adjustment bases; unknown units are excluded from homogeneous estimates. |
| AC97 | P0 | Competitive or people claim: verify entity/role/date associations, distinguish capability/use/outcome, and narrow unverified absence or superiority to the actual evidence. |
| AC98 | P0 | Financial ranking: align horizons, costs, taxes, vacancy and reinvestment assumptions; test joint adverse scenarios before claiming robustness and condition conclusions when inputs remain unknown. |
| AC99 | P0 | Frozen delivery: detect missing/extra/changed files and broken local destinations, test relocated outputs, retain original PDFs and issue derivatives; missing/unsupported checks are explicit partials. |
| AC100 | P0 | Transcript preservation: copy only explicitly provided accessible exports, retain exact bytes with origin/hash receipts, reject overwrite, and state host-export completeness limits. |

## Automatic deeper-investigation suggestions

| ID | Priority | Scenario and observable acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| AC101 | P1 | Research-cycle delivery automatically presents deeper-investigation suggestions in chat after the analytical index, without another request or a file-only menu; no strong candidates produces a brief explicit statement, not filler or a fixed quota. |
| AC102 | P1 | Each proposal links its actual finding/gap, states the question, decision value and incremental scope, and explains relative effort, evidence route and material access limits; hypotheses remain hypotheses and proposal priority is distinct from confidence. |
| AC103 | P1 | Candidates are filtered against confirmed objectives, overlap, impact, relevant uncertainty and answerability versus effort; a recommended subset has a rationale without an invented numerical ranking, and generation itself performs no new collection. |
| AC104 | P0 | Errors, routine verification and unfinished approved work remain current-cycle obligations, never optional upsells; new scope waits for user selection within an authorized mandate while delivery completes without waiting. |
| AC105 | P1 | Delivered proposals and rationale survive in the cycle journal, linked from README; subsequent selection, deferral or rejection preserves the original record, and no proposal is mislabeled an executed study. |
| AC106 | P0 | Every file in `04-data/` except its top-level `README.md` and content-verified OS/iCloud metadata has a `PROVENANCE.json` entry citing at least one real `03-sources/` capture and a method (cited script or description); a cited script is exempt; legacy dossiers are backfilled honestly or report the gap; the delivery check fails otherwise. |

Structural tests can verify packaging, files and provenance. They cannot prove a future model will honor these instructions or that the resulting research is useful. Proposal behavior has been observed in exploratory runs, but not every scenario is cleared; historical evaluations do not validate all future executions.
