# Conclusion audit

An audit changes what can be concluded. It is not a disclaimer appended to an unchanged recommendation.

## Check the premises that carry the answer

Before closing, select the factual claims, calculations and comparative assertions whose failure would materially change the answer. Include decisive secondary findings, such as an executive's role or a valuation, when they are reported as established. Use the [claim-check record](../assets/claim-check.md), scaled to the decision rather than a fixed number of citations.

For each selected claim, reopen the original source or preserved original bytes and identify the exact passage, table row or page. Keep the source statement distinct from the inference. A tool-generated extraction is a derivative: re-reading it is not a second verification. Check that entity, role, geography, date, product version and field-limit scope belong to the same row or statement. Preserve a short relevant excerpt or precise locator alongside the original capture. If the original is inaccessible, label the claim extraction-only/unverified and narrow its use; do not invent direct access.

Record **what changed in the answer**: keep, condition, correct, withdraw, or not verified. A failed premise must change dependent numbers, executive claims and recommendations, not just the confidence score. Never broaden a range to conceal an invalid endpoint. Recheck the revised conclusion against the surviving evidence.

## Apply the checks that fit the claim

- **Rates and comparables:** establish the numerator, denominator definition, population and date before aggregating. Open the underlying records behind decisive directory or index figures; retain availability, condition and field definitions. A minimum or one available offer is not a population average. Unknown or incompatible area/unit bases stay outside a homogeneous sample; they can be shown separately as exploratory evidence. Deduplicate by underlying entity/transaction, retaining uncertain clusters and sensitivity. The same observation divided by a different area is not independent low-price evidence. An adjustment for condition, parking, age or quality needs a verified difference and evidence for the adjustment; otherwise leave it qualitative. A gap between unmatched population statistics does not measure a negotiated discount. Asking prices do not establish a transaction floor or time-to-sale probability.
- **Financial comparisons:** distinguish gross arithmetic, net cash flow, total return and spendable income. Start alternatives from the same wealth and horizon; state whether terminal assets are retained or liquidated. Reconcile ending wealth, including principal consumed by transaction costs, under that convention before comparing returns. Show break-even conditions with unknown taxes, selling costs, vacancy, timing and future rates as explicit parameters. Test plausible adverse assumptions jointly before claiming a ranking is robust. A yield-to-maturity or current policy rate is not automatically a stable withdrawal rate. When decisive inputs are unknown, give conditional scenarios instead of an unconditional personalized recommendation.
- **Competitive claims:** distinguish a published capability, named production use, independently verified outcome and observed competition. For a decisive product comparison, inspect each side's relevant documentation or worked examples for the same task: prerequisites, limits, extension paths and deployment conditions. A component limit is not automatically a platform ceiling; a marketing promise does not establish the other product's superiority. Before an absence claim, check relevant product pages, release notes, examples and accessible technical docs; narrow an incomplete search to “not verified in the sources checked.” A named customer base is not automatically the number of users served by an agent; a write-back into a system is not proof that the system was replaced. A marketplace/private-offer anchor is not a verified minimum contract or evidence that a customer segment is protected. Qualify advantages by the specific tested/documented dimension.
- **People and capital:** verify each person/role/region association together, not by aligning independent lists. Separate round proceeds, cumulative capital, secondary sales, revenue/run-rate and cash on hand. Reconcile discrepancies and avoid unsupported qualifiers such as pre-/post-money. Repeated announcement coverage does not add independent confirmation.

These are conditional checks, not mandatory research topics. Do not expand a brief merely to fill the template. When direct verification is impossible within the approved effort, deliver the supported remainder with the invalid conclusion removed.

## Five checks

1. **Evidence and access:** verify load-bearing claims against accessible source text. Distinguish an original, extract, snippet, inaccessible link, vendor claim and independent observation. Citation presence is not entailment; repeated releases are not independent evidence.
2. **Units, populations and calculations:** check denominators, inclusion criteria, duplicate observations, dates, currencies, comparisons and the arithmetic behind the displayed table. Requested price is not sale price; interior area is not automatically private or total area; a payment promise is not a payment.
3. **Inference and counterevidence:** identify assumptions, causal leaps, missing comparators and the strongest plausible alternative. Search for a claimed absence before relying on it. Apply the same scrutiny to the user's product or preferred hypothesis.
4. **Recommendation and confidence:** trace the recommendation to its weakest material premise. An unresolved central gap prevents high confidence in a conclusion that depends on it. Narrow, condition, correct or withdraw the recommendation. A wider numerical interval still needs evidence.
5. **Sufficiency and synthesis:** explain how the answer resolves what the brief requires the user to understand or decide. Read each decisive executive claim alongside its supporting study: preserve the conditions and counterevidence that bound it. “Not found” must not become “absent,” or a provisional scenario an expected outcome, through compression. An accurate inventory can still fail the question. Recover omitted analysis from existing studies before collecting more.

## Dispositions

| Finding | Required action |
|---|---|
| Supported within scope | Keep with correct source and bounds |
| Conditional | Put the condition beside the conclusion |
| Contradicted or incorrect | Correct or remove, then revise dependencies |
| Insufficient support | Investigate if feasible within the mandate; otherwise remove precision or report partial |
| True facts, inadequate answer | Recover relevant explanation or investigate the central gap within scope; do not certify completeness by word/source count |

Recheck affected claims after fixes; do not restart the entire research automatically. Routine correction within the mandate needs no new gate. Material expansions follow delivery.

## Confidence

Use the shared 0–100 judgment scale in steps of ten, with evidence basis and revision condition. Do not produce a weighted global average or a probability of business success. A primary announcement may strongly support what was announced and weakly support effectiveness. User-provided premises remain labeled assumptions. If the evidence cannot support a score, state **not assessable with available evidence** and explain why.

## Independence and records

Self-review is the baseline, not a blind independent audit. If a separate reviewer is authorized and available, provide the brief, draft and necessary evidence without prescribing its verdict; account for its cost. Preserve its original response before making edits. Record which parts were reviewed by an author versus another researcher. If independent review is required but unavailable, disclose that missing coverage.

Store verification actions, material corrections and remaining gaps in the cycle's review records. Do not claim to preserve inaccessible model context or hidden reasoning. Mechanical checks establish files, links, versions or page counts, never factual truth or user satisfaction.

## Completion

Confirm every primary and secondary objective has an explicit coverage state. Verify files exist, original handoffs were preserved or gaps declared, and the latest pointer refers to a coherent published packet. A source or rendering failure may yield a partial delivery; it must not leave the user unknowingly waiting for input.
