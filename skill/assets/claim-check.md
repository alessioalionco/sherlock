# Central-claim verification

Cycle and report/study version: fill from the current research. Reviewer: actual identity and role; identify self-review. Save under the cycle's `reviews/` directory. Select claims that materially support the answer and important factual assertions presented as established; do not create one row per citation.

| Claim ID and dependent conclusion | Original source / captured version / precise locator | Source statement and access level | Verification | Disposition and propagated change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exact claim, including entity/unit/date/scope; identify affected study, table and executive section | URL plus local original path and page/row/heading | Short relevant excerpt or locator; distinguish original, extraction-only, snippet, inaccessible | Independent arithmetic, same-row association, competing explanation, or direct source check; state what was actually checked | Keep / condition / correct / withdraw / not verified; specify actual edits and remaining bounds |

For rates: record numerator, denominator basis, eligible population, duplicate clusters and calculation. For comparative claims: separate declared capability, observed deployment/outcome and inference. Record a substantive second-source check only if it adds independent evidence; repeating a generated extraction does not.

Closing assessment: state whether the main answer survives, the weakest remaining premise, coverage, confidence with basis, and any conclusion removed. Verify that the executive, full report, study and final conclusion agree after corrections. Unknowns remain unknown; this record is not a truth certificate or a new scope-approval gate.
