# First-delivery template

Adapt this structure to the user's language and actual artifacts. Replace fields with verified values; omit unavailable formats with a concise explanation. Do not announce planned files as delivered.

Research folder: **[Research name](folder-path)** · [Start here](README-path)

Completed the research on **subject**, covering the agreed primary and secondary objectives.

- **[Executive report](executive-path)** — actual page count, if verified.
- **[Full report](full-report-path)** — total and analytical page counts, if verified.
- **[Study library](study-index-path)** — individual studies.

**Central conclusion:** direct answer, practical implication, confidence with its basis, and the limitation that materially changes interpretation.

**Organization:** brief numbered themes with their principal finding or explicit partial/unanswered state. Identify secondary objectives; do not let them disappear into a generic overview.

| File | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Linked study title | Specific question and scope |
| Further studies | Include every analytical document, without duplicate format rows |
| Full file inventory | Every source, dataset, original agent handoff, review and historical record |
| **Final conclusion** | **Consolidated assessment, recommendation, confidence, limits and revision conditions** |

**Suggested deeper investigations — proposed next cycle, not yet executed**

Include this block automatically after the analytical index. Offer only strong opportunities; there is no fixed count. If none remains, say so briefly. Use compact numbered entries with this shape:

1. **Topic — research question.** Trigger: [finding or recorded gap](study-or-journal-path). Value: the decision or understanding this could change. Increment: what this would add to the current answer. Feasibility/effort: likely evidence route, relative effort and reason, including access limits.

**Suggested priorities:** a small subset and why it is worth investigating first, when useful. Keep corrections or unfinished approved work separate, with their current-cycle status. Preserve these proposals in the cycle journal and link that record from the dossier README.

Offer relevant conversational exploration or derived formats. The completed delivery does not wait for a response; deeper investigations proceed only after user selection within an authorized mandate.

For hundreds of raw files, the chat links the inventory rather than listing them individually. Link PDF, HTML and Markdown as formats of the same document. Link the study near a substantive claim when deeper analysis exists; source citations remain distinct.
